About Our Faculty

Excellence in ICT Education Since 2005

The Faculty of Information and Communication Technology at Mangosuthu University of Technology (MUT) is a leading provider of ICT education in KwaZulu-Natal. Located in the vibrant community of Umlazi, Durban, we have been producing industry-ready graduates since 2005.

Our faculty is staffed by highly qualified academics and industry professionals who bring real-world experience from leading tech companies. We maintain strong partnerships with Microsoft, Cisco, and local tech enterprises to ensure our curriculum stays relevant and cutting-edge.

We pride ourselves on our state-of-the-art facilities, including enterprise networking labs with Cisco equipment, modern programming labs, and 24/7 computer access. Our students benefit from hands-on learning, industry internships, and strong career placement support.

Software Development Networking & Security Data Management Cybersecurity Cloud Computing Web Technologies

Expert Academic Staff

Learn from PhD-holding professors and industry-certified instructors with experience at Google, IBM, Microsoft, and Cisco Systems.

Modern Laboratory Facilities

Access enterprise-grade networking labs with Cisco equipment, programming labs with dual monitors, and high-speed fibre internet.

Industry Partnerships

Strong ties with Microsoft, Cisco, MTN, Vodacom, and local tech startups provide internship and employment opportunities.
