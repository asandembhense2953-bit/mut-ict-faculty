Admissions

Apply to MUT ICT

Learn the entry requirements, how to apply, and how to prepare your application for the next academic intake.

The Faculty of ICT accepts applications through the Central Applications Office (CAO). Apply online at cao.ac.za and make sure your documentation is ready before the deadline.

Diploma in ICT

National Senior Certificate with diploma admission. Minimum 50% Mathematics or 60% Mathematical Literacy, 50% English.

Extended Curriculum

National Senior Certificate. Minimum 40% Mathematics or 50% Mathematical Literacy, 40% English. Includes a foundation academic year.

Advanced Diploma

Requires a Diploma in ICT or related qualification (NQF Level 6), with a 60% average and motivation letter.

Requirements

Entry Requirements

Diploma

Minimum requirements

  • National Senior Certificate with diploma endorsement
  • Mathematics 50% or Mathematical Literacy 60%
  • English 50%
  • Physical Sciences recommended
Extended

Minimum requirements

  • National Senior Certificate
  • Mathematics 40% or Mathematical Literacy 50%
  • English 40%
  • Academic support and foundation year included
Advanced Diploma

Minimum requirements

  • Diploma in ICT or related qualification
  • Minimum 60% average in prior studies
  • Motivation letter
  • Interview may be required
How to Apply

Application Guidance

Apply via CAO

Submit your application through cao.ac.za for official intake.

Prepare documents

Have your NSC, ID, and previous academic records ready. Include a motivation letter for the Advanced Diploma.

Need help?

Contact the department at ict@mut.ac.za or call +27 31 123 4567 for guidance.
